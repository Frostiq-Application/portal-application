# Changelog — Frostique Portal

Everything we have shipped, newest first, written in plain words. This is the list your
team sees inside the portal after an update, so keep it readable for a bakery owner.

**How the numbers work**

- **1.0.0 → 2.0.0** — a big release that changes how something already works.
- **1.0.0 → 1.1.0** — new things to use.
- **1.0.0 → 1.0.1** — fixes and small touch-ups only.

**How to add an entry**

Add a new block at the top with the version number and the release date, then list the
changes under **Added**, **Improved** and **Fixed**. Write about what a person can now do
on screen, not about the code.

---

## 1.0.0 — 20 August 2026

The first release. Everything below is what the portal does on day one.

### Getting started

- Sign up for a bakery account, confirm the email address with a code, and reset a
  forgotten password.
- A setup walkthrough that takes a new owner from an empty account to a working shop.
- Pick a plan, see what each one includes side by side, and pay from the portal.
- Team members who were invited set their password from the emailed link.

### Dashboard and reports

- Dashboard with the day's numbers, counted up as they load.
- Reports with a date range to choose from: sales, top products, busiest slots and what
  customers are saving to their wishlists.
- Reports that a plan does not include are shown, with a short note on what unlocks them.

### Orders

- Live order queue — new orders land on screen on their own, with a sound.
- Day tabs, sorting and filters to work through the queue.
- Open an order in a side panel with the items, the customer, how to reach them, the
  branch and the delivery address.
- Write up an order at the counter, look the caller up by phone number, or add them as a
  new customer without leaving the form.

### Kitchen and delivery screens

- Two full-screen boards built for a wall display or a tablet on the counter — one for
  the kitchen, one for delivery — with large type and a detail panel per order.

### Custom cakes

- A queue of custom cake requests with the customer's photos, details and date.
- Raise a request yourself for a customer who called or walked in.
- Move a request along one clear status at a time; accepting it creates the order.
- A dot on the sidebar when a new request arrives, from wherever you are in the portal.

### Menu

- Categories, products, options and add-ons, per branch, with images compressed as they
  are uploaded and progress shown.
- Upload a whole menu from a spreadsheet, download a sample file to fill in, and see
  exactly which rows failed.
- Copy a menu from one branch to another, in full or picking what to bring across.
- A starter menu offered on a branch that is still empty.

### Design gallery

- Manage the photos each branch shows customers as "make it like this" examples.

### Storefront content

- Edit the brand: name, logo, banner, about text and social links.
- Banners with a link to open and a drag to reorder them.
- Announcements, and switching an occasion on or off in the featured products.
- Choose which country the storefront's phone field starts on.

### Branches, scheduling and coupons

- Add and edit branches, set who on the team can reach each one, and see the limit the
  plan allows.
- Set opening days, slots, how many orders a slot takes and blocked dates — one schedule
  per branch.
- Create coupons, decide which branches they apply to, and switch them on or off.

### Customers and enquiries

- A customer directory with contact details, order history and lifetime spend.
- Enquiries from the website in one list, each with a status to move along.

### Team

- Invite people by email, choose the role and which branches they can reach.
- Ready-made roles plus custom roles where you pick the permissions yourself.
- Turn a team member's access on or off.

### Your subscription

- See the plan you are on, what it includes, and what you are using against its limits.
- Change plan or billing cycle, with the amount worked out and explained before you pay,
  including credit for what you have already paid for.
- Buy add-ons, apply a coupon, activate a trial, or cancel.
- Invoices and payment history, and a clear screen while a payment is going through.
- When a limit is reached, the portal explains what happened and what would lift it,
  instead of just refusing.

### Platform administration

- Every bakery account in one place, with its plan, usage and branches.
- Create and edit plans, billing cycles, features and subscription coupons.
- Grant or remove a single feature for one account.
- Billing settings, platform-wide numbers, the activity log, and the tools for setting up
  an account's data space.

### Look and feel

- Sidebar that collapses to icons, brand splash while the portal loads, one date picker
  used everywhere, and a single icon set across every screen.
